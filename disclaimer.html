Fictional people and conversations

Participant names, voices and chat messages are invented. Any resemblance to real people or real meetings is coincidental.

The one exception is the meeting with audio. The two people on camera there are real volunteers filmed for a research dataset. They are not the people named in the call, and they never took part in this site. The colleagues with their cameras off speak with computer-generated voices.
