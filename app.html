Get the app

Totally Real Meeting, right on your iPhone

Add it to your Home Screen and it opens just like an app: full screen, without the address bar, one tap away. Setup takes about 20 seconds.

Full honesty: this isn’t an actual app. It’s this website, pinned to your iPhone with its own icon and no browser bars. But it Totally looks like one, and that’s the whole idea behind Totally Real Meeting.

You’re already using the app version. Nothing more to do here.

  1. Open the site in Safari

    On your iPhone, open Safari and go to:

    https://openeli.github.io/totally-real-meeting/

    Once the page opens, Safari’s address bar at the bottom shows only openeli.github.io. That’s normal.

    openeli.github.io

  2. Press and hold the address bar, then tap Share

    Put your finger on the address bar (where it says openeli.github.io) and keep it there for a second. A menu pops up above it. Tap Share, the first option.

  3. Tap Add to Home Screen

    A panel slides up from the bottom. Scroll down its list and tap Add to Home Screen, the option with a plus sign in a square.

    Don’t see it? Keep scrolling, since it sits near the end of the list. If the list ends with View More, tap that first.

  4. Keep Open as Web App on, then tap Add

    The name can stay Real Meeting, or you can change it. Make sure Open as Web App is switched on (green), then tap Add at the top right.

  5. Open it from your Home Screen

    Find the Real Meeting icon on your Home Screen and tap it. It opens full screen, without the address bar, and keeps the settings you saved on the website.

On Android

  1. Open Chrome and go to https://openeli.github.io/totally-real-meeting/.
  2. Tap ⋮ (three dots) to the right of the address bar.
  3. Tap Add to home screen. On some phones it says Install app.
  4. Tap Install, then confirm. The Real Meeting icon appears on your home screen.

Added it before?

An icon saved before this site supported web app mode may still open in Safari. Remove it from your Home Screen, then add it again with the steps above.

On a computer

Chrome and Edge show an install button at the right end of the address bar. Click it to open the site in its own window.
