Get the app

Totally Real Meeting, right on your iPhone

Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like any other app: full screen, without the address bar, one tap away. Setup takes about 20 seconds.

You’re already using the app version. Nothing more to do here.

  1. Open this page in Safari

    On your iPhone, open this site in Safari. You’ll find the address bar at the bottom of the screen.

    openeli.github.io

  2. Touch and hold the address bar

    Keep your finger on the address bar until a small menu pops up, then tap Share.

    Prefer buttons? Tap ••• next to the address bar, then Share.

  3. Scroll down to Add to Home Screen

    A sheet slides up with ways to share. Scroll the list a little and tap Add to Home Screen.

  4. Keep “Open as Web App” on, then tap Add

    You can rename the icon if you like. Leave Open as Web App switched on (that’s what gives it the full-screen look) and tap Add in the top corner.

  5. Open it from your Home Screen

    Look for Real Meeting among your apps. It opens full screen and remembers your settings, just like the website.

Added it before?

An icon saved before this site became an app may still open in Safari. Remove it from your Home Screen and add it again with the steps above.

On Android

In Chrome, tap ⋮ at the top right, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

On a computer

Chrome and Edge show an install button in the address bar. Click it to open the site in its own window.
